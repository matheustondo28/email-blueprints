Our story

Two friends,
one car, no plan.

It started on Route 66. Two friends from Barcelona, a rented car, and a search for jewelry that felt like the trip did: loud, a little worn, free. They couldn't find it, so they came home and made it.

Every piece is still drawn in Barcelona and cast in solid 925 sterling silver. It picks up scratches. It is supposed to.
